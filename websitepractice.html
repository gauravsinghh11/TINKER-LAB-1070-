About sit nagpur

OVERVIEW OF DEPARTMENT The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Symbiosis Institute of Technology (SIT), Nagpur, offers an industry-focused 4-year B.Tech program structured around the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. The curriculum provides specialized honors tracks in high-demand fields like Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Cloud Computing. Students benefit from global academic opportunities, including 2+2 dual-degree pathways with prestigious international universities like Deakin University, Australia.The department features modern, tech-driven infrastructure designed to foster hands-on experiential learning. Students train in advanced computing laboratories, smart air-conditioned classrooms, and collaborative workspaces that host regular hackathons, tech workshops, and research projects. Beyond pure technical training, the campus provides premium residential hostel facilities, extensive sports amenities, and mandatory service-learning modules to ensure all-round student development.Admission to the CSE program is merit-based, evaluating candidates through national and institutional entrance exams like SITEEE, JEE Main, or MHT CET. The department places a strong emphasis on career readiness, integrating mandatory industry internships and professional capstones directly into the credit structure. This rigorous approach prepares graduates for strong placement opportunities with top-tier global tech firms and engineering enterprises.
